# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Stopwatch pill under the document title, shaped to match the Add Template pill,
  with hands that sweep one revolution a minute while running.
- Two tone lap chip beside it: lap number on the left, `h:mm:ss` on the right,
  with an option for milliseconds.
- Lap, Pause, Resume and Stop. Stop records the part lap that was in progress.
- Optional top bar stopwatch, for starting a run without the document open.
- Laps recorded against each document's key, rolling up through every folder
  above it to a single total for everything.
- "Laps" in the right click menu of any Rem and in the document menu, scoped to
  that item and everything below it.
- Stats page with a lap list (edit and delete per row) and an interactive chart
  of either time per day or individual lap times, over any date range.
- Scope picker over the rollup tree, for reporting on a document, a folder, or
  everything.
- Ten colour shades with authored light and dark pairs, separate shades for the
  fastest and slowest lap, and an intensity control.
- Omnibar commands for the stats page, the rolled up total, the settings in
  effect, and a debug report.
